Prototype

A clickable prototype of our converged solution: one app that carries a client's precise haircut spec between chairs — and lets both sides see the cut before it happens.

Merged from two concepts
Concept 1 · Hair Profile Record (spec + history + QR)
Augmented Photo Preview (visual try-on + confidence)
Build profile → Preview new style → Lock & share QR → Barber reads spec + rates confidence → 360° update after the cut
1Client: open profile, try a new style, adjust length & fade, lock it, show QR.
2Barber: scan QR → full spec, dye alert, target preview → rate confidence → suggest adjustment if unsure.
3After the cut: record 360° to update the profile — ready for any future barber.
